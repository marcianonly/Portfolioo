Passive Through-Wall Human Activity Recognition (HAR) using Ambient Wi-Fi Beacons
May 2025 | Research & Development

Developed a first-of-its-kind Passive Sensing Framework that uses ordinary Wi-Fi routers to detect human movement through walls without cameras or wearable devices.

Key Innovation: Signal Reconstruction Pipeline

  • Audio Engineering Approach: Used Cubic Spline Interpolation to upsample sparse 10Hz Wi-Fi signals into smooth 100Hz waveforms
  • Privacy-First: Captured management frames in Promiscuous Mode, requiring zero network authentication.
